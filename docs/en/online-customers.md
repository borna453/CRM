# 1. Online customers

In this overview, you can see which customers are currently online or have recently been online.
This provides you with insight into their recent activity on the platform, 
allowing you to quickly respond to their needs or questions. For example, 
you can reach out to a customer while they are online to offer immediate support or discuss business matters. 
This helps you better address their needs and optimize customer interactions.
