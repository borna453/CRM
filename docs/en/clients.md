# Client overview
Here you can find an overview of all your client, including their associated open tasks and opportunities. 
This gives you a complete view of ongoing projects and possibilities for each client.

# 2. Add client
You can easily add new customers via the 'Add Client' button on the right-hand side of the screen.
This allows you to quickly create client profiles and start working with them immediately.

# 3. Filter client
At the top of the page, you can choose between Active and Archived clients. 
This provides a clear distinction between clients you are currently working
with and those whose relationship has been completed or paused, helping you maintain an organized view of all client relationships.
