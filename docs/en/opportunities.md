# 1. Register Opportunities
Click on the 'Create Opportunity' button on the right to register a new opportunity. 
Enter the key details such as the company, the opportunity's subject, an optional description, 
expected revenue, and costs. This information serves as the foundation for managing opportunities 
within your pipeline and helps track future deals and business possibilities.
Fill in the necessary details, such as:

Company
Subject
Description (optional)
Expected Revenue
Costs

Vul hierbij de benodigde gegevens in zoals:
Bedrijf, Onderwerp, Beschrijving (optioneel), Verwachte omzet, Kosten

# 2. Add Notes
Add relevant notes to the opportunity to capture additional details. 
These notes can include useful information such as agreements with the client, 
specific requirements, or other details that may influence the progress of the opportunity. 
This way, you maintain a complete overview of all relevant information related to the opportunity.

# 3. Add Tasks
To effectively manage the progress of the opportunity, you can add specific tasks. 
These tasks help organize the actions that need to be taken to realize the opportunity, 
such as follow-up actions or client interactions. By adding tasks, you stay organized and ensure that nothing is overlooked.
