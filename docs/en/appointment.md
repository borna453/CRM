# 1. Month View
View all your appointments in a clear month view. 
This provides a complete overview of your schedule for the entire month, 
allowing you to see your appointments and available time at a glance.

# 2. Week View / Day View
In these views, you can check your appointments on a weekly or daily basis. 
The week view provides a detailed overview of your weekly schedule, 
while the day view offers a specific look at your daily appointments, ideal for precise time management.

# 3. Directly in the Calendar
Easily schedule an appointment by clicking directly on a day in the calendar. 
In the week or day view, you can also select a specific time to plan and manage your appointments more accurately.

# 4. Appointment List
All appointments are displayed in a clear list format. 
This helps you quickly see your appointments in sequence, 
and if necessary, you can also add new appointments directly from this list.

# 5. Top Menu
In the top menu, you will find a lightning symbol, allowing you to quickly and easily schedule a new appointment, 
no matter where you are in the calendar. This ensures a fast and efficient way to add appointments.
