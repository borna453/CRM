# 1. Add Customers
Easily add new customers to your system using this option. 
Fill in all relevant customer details to create a complete profile that you can reference and manage later.

# 2. Set Financial Goals
Here you can set financial goals and track them accurately. 
This allows you to monitor your business objectives and performance, making adjustments where necessary.

# 3. Customize Templates
Customize templates for notifications and documents to align them with your business needs. 
This ensures that all communications remain professional and consistent, 
tailored to the specific needs of your clients and your brand.

# 4. Appointments Overview
The dashboard provides an overview of all your appointments, 
allowing you to quickly review your schedule and manage appointments efficiently.

# 5. Customer Overview
The dashboard also includes an overview of all your customers, giving you immediate access to their information, 
history, and ongoing appointments. This helps you work quickly and effectively with your customers.

# 6. Track Activities
Track and manage customer activities here. 
This provides insight into their interactions and helps you strengthen 
relationships by responding quickly to their needs and behaviors.

# 7. Task Overview
View and manage your tasks in this overview. You can prioritize tasks, assign them, and set deadlines
to ensure that all actions are handled efficiently.

# 8. Opportunities Overview
Here, you will get an overview of your opportunities. This gives you insights into potential deals 
and opportunities your business can leverage, allowing you to act proactively and drive growth.
