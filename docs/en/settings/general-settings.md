# 1. Personalize

You can visually customize your account by adding various images, such as a logo for both dark mode and light mode, 
ensuring that your branding looks consistent in both views. Additionally, you can also customize the favicon of the site.

# Favicons
A favicon is a small icon displayed in the browser tab next to the page title.
It helps users quickly recognize your website when multiple tabs are open. 
It is a small but important part of your visual identity and contributes to the professional appearance of your website.
